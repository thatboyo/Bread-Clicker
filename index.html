How to Play

  • Click on the bread to get bread, each bread sells for 1 dollar
  • Spend Bread on helpers to the right, they can bake bread automatically for you,
  • Buy a crafting Table, villager(WIP), or command block(WIP) for helpful upgrades
  • Use the menu to save and load, or transfer your save code to a new device
